# Accelerate response and remediation in the Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI) | Microsoft Learn

This pillar focuses on reducing incident impact and duration through faster mitigation, stronger coordination, and continuous learning.

## Security outcome Microsoft prioritized

Microsoft prioritized reducing time to mitigation for high-severity risk and improving resilience by integrating incident lessons into operational and engineering controls.

## What Microsoft prioritized

- Faster vulnerability mitigation and remediation workflows
- Better response orchestration and role clarity
- Stronger incident communication and transparency discipline
- Improved feedback loops from incidents into engineering and governance

## Lessons from SFI implementation

- Incident response maturity depends on both technical automation and operational governance.
- Faster remediation requires strong ownership models and prioritization discipline.
- Post-incident learning has to drive durable changes in architecture, standards, and engineering workflows.

## Zero Trust and NIST CSF alignment

This pillar primarily applies **Verify explicitly**, **Use least privilege access**, and **Assume breach**.

Representative NIST CSF mappings:

- **Respond (RS)** for incident triage, analysis, and mitigation
- **Recover (RC)** for restoration and resilience improvement
- **Govern (GV)** for accountability and sustained improvement
